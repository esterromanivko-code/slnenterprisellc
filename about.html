We Started Small.
We Stay Intentional.

SLN Enterprise LLC was founded on a simple belief: professional cleaning shouldn't feel like a gamble. Too many people had hired services that showed up late, skipped corners, or sent different unfamiliar faces every time with no consistency or accountability.

We built something different. A team that shows up on time, follows a thorough process on every visit, and actually communicates clearly with clients. Not just a cleaning crew — a reliable partner for your home or business.

We've grown through word of mouth, one satisfied client at a time. And we plan to keep it that way — doing quality work, standing behind every clean, and treating every space we enter with the respect it deserves.

Our Commitment

The SLN Promise

We're not just trying to show up and check boxes. We're trying to be the cleaning service you actually stick with — the one you tell your friends about.

  • If you're not satisfied, we return within 24 hours and make it right — no questions, no charge.

  • We never send unchecked strangers to your home. Every team member is vetted and trained directly by us.

  • Your pricing is locked in. No surprise fees, no bait-and-switch quotes after the first visit.

  • Reschedule, adjust, or cancel with reasonable notice — no complicated hoops or penalties.

  • We bring every supply and piece of equipment. You don't need to provide a single thing.
