Questions About Pricing

Is this estimate the final price?

It's a real range based on our standard rates, and most homes land inside it. We confirm the final number once we've seen the condition of the space — and we always tell you before anything changes.

Why is deep cleaning more expensive?

A deep clean takes roughly twice as long. It covers inside appliances, baseboards, door frames, light fixtures, grout and the build-up a regular clean doesn't reach. Most first-time clients start here, then move to standard visits.

How do the recurring discounts work?

A home that's cleaned regularly takes less time, so we pass that back: 5% off monthly, 10% off bi-weekly, 15% off weekly. The discount applies to every visit, and you can pause or cancel any time.

Do I need to be home?

No. Plenty of our clients leave a key or door code and come home to a clean house. We're fully insured and bonded, so your home is in good hands either way.

How long does a cleaning take?

Standard cleans usually run 2–3 hours depending on size. Deep cleans and move-out cleans typically take 4–6 hours.

Which areas do you serve?

King County including Seattle, Bellevue, Renton, Kent, Auburn, Kirkland and Redmond; Pierce County including Tacoma and Puyallup; and Snohomish County including Everett and Lynnwood.
